Application & Privacy

MailLedger (v1.3.0) is an open-source, 100% local email audit ledger for Mozilla Thunderbird released under the MIT License. It records message subjects, senders, received dates, and exact deletion timestamps in Thunderbird's local storage without ever reading email bodies or making external network requests.
